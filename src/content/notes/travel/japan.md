## Tokyo

- Get a Suica or Pasmo card immediately
- Convenience store food is genuinely good
- Shibuya at night is overwhelming in the best way

## Kyoto

- Fushimi Inari early morning to avoid crowds
- The Philosopher's Path is worth a slow walk

## Random tips

- Pocket Wi-Fi or eSIM is essential
- Cash is still king in many places
- Learn basic phrases; it goes a long way
