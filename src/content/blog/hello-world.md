This is the first post on my new site. I have been writing notes in private for years. It is time to put some of them in public.

## Why write in public?

Writing forces clarity. If you cannot explain an idea simply, you probably do not understand it yet. Publishing adds a small pressure that makes the writing better.

## What to expect

I will write about:

- Software architecture and backend systems
- Tools I build and lessons from shipping them
- Travel notes and books I am reading

Most posts will be short and practical. If a topic needs depth, I will split it into a series.

## Colophon

This site is built with **Astro**, written in **Markdown**, and deployed on a VPS. The design is intentionally minimal: fast to load, easy to read, and cheap to maintain.

Thanks for reading.
